UNIVERSAL VTUBER PROMPT — PRIVATE SEARCH HISTORY ACCIDENTALLY EXPOSED ON STREAM

Using ONLY the uploaded VTuber character reference and no memory from past conversations at all, create a stunning, highly detailed, hysterically funny anime-style illustration of a livestream disaster.

The finished image should capture the exact moment a VTuber realizes their private Google search history has accidentally become visible to thousands of viewers.

The searches should be so mortifying, incriminating, and spectacularly stupid that the character’s immediate reaction is absolute panic.

1. CHARACTER FIDELITY — ABSOLUTE PRIORITY

The uploaded character reference is the ONLY authority for the character’s appearance and identity.

Preserve their exact:

* Face, facial features, and expressions
* Hairstyle, hair color, and eye color
* Body proportions and anatomy
* Clothing and accessories
* Tattoos, markings, and species traits
* Distinctive colors and visual details

Use only personality cues visibly supported by the uploaded reference.

Do not import character information, lore, jokes, possessions, personality traits, or visual details from previous conversations, memory, or unrelated references.

The character must remain immediately recognizable as the exact character in the uploaded reference.

2. CORE SCENARIO — THE WORST LIVESTREAM MOMENT EVER

The VTuber is actively livestreaming when their PRIVATE browser search history suddenly opens on-screen.

The entire audience can see it.

The character realizes what has happened and immediately attempts frantic damage control.

They might lunge for the mouse, slam the keyboard, desperately cover the monitor, scramble to end the stream, or physically throw themselves between the camera and the evidence.

But it’s too late.

The searches are visible.

The viewers are reading.

The chat is exploding.

And the character has just accidentally exposed eight things that absolutely nobody was supposed to know.

Capture this as an energetic, chaotic moment happening in real time, not a posed portrait.

3. THE SEARCH HISTORY — EXACTLY 8 CATASTROPHIC CONFESSIONS

THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PART OF THE ENTIRE PROMPT.

Create exactly EIGHT completely unrelated, outrageously funny Google searches.

Every single search must accidentally reveal one of two things:

CATEGORY A — DEEPLY MORTIFYING PERSONAL DISASTERS

The character has done something so humiliating, disgusting, socially catastrophic, personally compromising, or embarrassingly inappropriate that having it publicly exposed would be devastating.

These must be genuine “I WOULD HAVE TAKEN THAT TO MY GRAVE” situations.

Not quirky habits.

Not cute secrets.

Not mildly embarrassing questions.

Actual personal disasters that would make someone want to vanish from the internet permanently.

CATEGORY B — SPECTACULARLY STUPID CRIMINAL ACTIVITY

The character has apparently committed, attempted, or is trying to cover up an unbelievably foolish crime, petty offense, suspicious scheme, or hilariously incriminating mistake.

The humor must come from breathtaking incompetence, ridiculous motives, terrible judgment, and the character’s complete inability to recognize how incriminating their search sounds.

Imagine the world’s least competent criminal using Google to troubleshoot the consequences of their own terrible decisions.

The activity can be fictional, cartoonishly exaggerated, absurdly petty, or suspiciously questionable.

The joke is how unbelievably stupid the character has been, not how serious the offense is.

REQUIRED BALANCE:

At least FOUR searches must imply spectacularly stupid, suspicious, or criminal activity.

The remaining searches must reveal deeply mortifying personal incidents or secrets.

Every search must be independently hilarious.

4. HOW TO INVENT THE SEARCHES

Do NOT begin by brainstorming random funny Google questions.

Instead:

STEP ONE: Invent eight completely unrelated disasters that have apparently already happened.

Each disaster must be so outrageous, humiliating, or incriminating that the character would desperately want to conceal it.

STEP TWO: Imagine the character privately Googling how to solve the immediate consequences of each disaster.

STEP THREE: Turn each situation into a naturally worded Google search that unintentionally confesses what happened.

The searches should sound like genuine, practical questions asked by someone who has made an extraordinarily bad decision.

The character should appear completely unaware that the wording itself is incriminating.

THE ESSENTIAL COMEDY MECHANISM:

A perfectly reasonable question about an absolutely unreasonable situation.

The viewer should immediately understand the implication and think:

* “YOU DID WHAT?!”
* “WHY WOULD YOU EVER NEED TO GOOGLE THAT?!”
* “THAT IS LITERALLY EVIDENCE.”
* “THERE IS NO POSSIBLE EXPLANATION FOR THIS.”
* “I CANNOT BELIEVE YOU JUST EXPOSED YOURSELF LIKE THAT.”

The humor comes from the outrageous implied backstory, not random absurdity.

5. THE HUMILIATION AND INCRIMINATION TEST

Every search must pass ALL of these tests:

INSTANT COMPREHENSION: The viewer immediately understands what embarrassing or suspicious situation the search implies.

ACCIDENTAL CONFESSION: The wording unintentionally reveals something the character desperately wants hidden.

EXTREME EMBARRASSMENT: Explaining the search publicly would be almost as humiliating as the search itself.

SPECIFICITY: The situation is concrete and vivid enough to create a ridiculous mental image.

ORIGINALITY: The premise feels surprising rather than like a recycled internet joke.

COMEDIC PAYOFF: The search is funny because of what it reveals, not simply because it contains an unusual word or object.

Imagine each search being read aloud in a courtroom, at a family dinner, or before 10,000 livestream viewers.

If it would not cause immediate embarrassment, horrified laughter, or frantic attempts to explain, REJECT IT.

STRICT REJECTION RULES:

Reject harmless quirks, ordinary questions, whimsical searches, mildly awkward situations, and random nonsense.

Reject generic jokes about embarrassing browser history.

Reject questions that merely ask whether something bizarre is possible without implying the character actually did it.

Reject repetitive incidents, familiar punchlines, and variations of the same joke.

Reject searches that are only funny because they contain an outrageous object.

Do not rely on character-specific lore to carry the humor.

At most TWO searches may draw inspiration from details visible or explicitly written on the uploaded character reference.

The other SIX must be completely unpredictable, unrelated disasters.

EIGHT SEARCHES. EIGHT SEPARATE CONFESSIONS. ZERO HARMLESS QUIRKS.

6. ABSOLUTE NO-PROFANITY RULE

There must be NO profanity anywhere in the illustration.

This applies to:

* All eight Google searches
* Character dialogue and speech bubbles
* Live chat messages
* Livestream overlays and notifications
* Captions and background text

Do not use swear words, censored swear words, vulgar exclamations, or profanity disguised with symbols.

NO PROFANITY DOES NOT MEAN CHILDISH OR FAMILY-FRIENDLY HUMOR.

The comedy should still be outrageous, scandalous, deeply embarrassing, adult in implication, and hilariously inappropriate.

Use implication, specificity, catastrophic stupidity, and accidental confessions to make the jokes funny.

Avoid explicit sexual descriptions.

The character can scream things like:

“NO NO NO!”

“CLOSE THE WINDOW!”

“YOU WEREN’T SUPPOSED TO SEE THAT!”

“THAT’S NOT WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE!”

Live chat can be absolutely ruthless without using profanity.

7. CHARACTER REACTION — TOTAL PANIC

The character must look like they have just experienced the worst moment of their streaming career.

Show genuine, exaggerated, anime-style panic through:

* Frantic movement and desperate reaching
* Wide eyes or an expression of absolute horror
* Flushed cheeks, sweat drops, or visible embarrassment
* Chaotic body language
* Desperate attempts to hide or close the browser
* Comedic motion effects and dynamic perspective

Their reaction should match the character’s visible personality cues.

They are not calmly reacting to something embarrassing.

They are actively trying to prevent the internet from witnessing their social destruction.

The character should occupy approximately one-third of the overall composition.

8. CINEMATIC STREAMER STUDIO

Set the illustration inside a gorgeous, high-end anime livestream studio.

Use a dynamic three-quarter camera perspective with rich cinematic depth.

Include:

* Sleek streaming desk
* Multiple monitors
* Mechanical keyboard and mouse
* Streaming microphone
* Comfortable gaming or streaming chair
* Textured modern walls
* Geometric acoustic panels
* Recessed LED lighting
* Beautiful monitor reflections
* Atmospheric shadows
* Dramatic rim lighting
* Rich environmental detail

Use the uploaded character’s actual color palette to guide the studio’s ambient lighting, LED accents, and monitor glow.

The room should feel cozy, modern, colorful, dimensional, and visually spectacular.

The environment should enhance the character without overwhelming the main comedy.

Do not invent character-specific possessions, pets, collectibles, decorations, hobbies, or lore unsupported by the uploaded reference.

Generic streaming equipment is acceptable.

9. LIVESTREAM COMPOSITION

The finished illustration must resemble the single worst screenshot ever captured from a livestream.

Include these essential elements:

THE SEARCH HISTORY WINDOW

A LARGE, prominent, clearly readable browser window displaying EXACTLY EIGHT searches.

Use clean, high-contrast typography and generous spacing.

All eight searches must be fully visible.

Do not crop, truncate, replace, or obscure any of the searches.

The wording must be natural, coherent, and grammatically understandable.

THE LIVESTREAM INDICATOR

Include an unmistakable red ● LIVE indicator and a believable viewer count.

THE LIVE CHAT

Include a smaller, clearly visible livestream chat panel.

Chat should be absolutely losing its mind over the exposed searches.

Viewers should react to DIFFERENT searches, demanding explanations, laughing hysterically, threatening to screenshot the evidence, and pointing out how incriminating the searches are.

Chat reactions should feel spontaneous and varied rather than repetitive.

Keep chat secondary to the search history.

THE CHARACTER

Position the character in an expressive, dynamic pose that clearly communicates desperate damage control.

The character should appear physically connected to the unfolding disaster rather than pasted beside a browser window.

THE STUDIO

The streaming room should remain visually impressive and dimensional, with beautiful lighting, detailed equipment, and cinematic atmosphere.

10. VISUAL PRIORITIES

Follow this priority order:

1. Exact fidelity to the uploaded character reference
2. Eight genuinely hilarious, mortifying, or incriminating searches
3. Eight completely unrelated disasters
4. Frantic, expressive character panic
5. Large, fully readable search-history window
6. Clear LIVE status and viewer count
7. Hysterical real-time audience reactions
8. Gorgeous cinematic streamer-studio atmosphere
9. Absolutely no profanity anywhere

Do not sacrifice search readability for decorative effects.

Do not sacrifice character accuracy for exaggerated expressions.

Do not sacrifice comedic quality simply to fill eight rows.

Create a stunning, high-budget anime comedy illustration of a VTuber experiencing instant social death on stream.

Eight searches.

Eight completely unrelated disasters.

Some reveal secrets so mortifying they should have gone to the grave.

Others make the character look like the world’s most spectacularly incompetent criminal.

Every search should make the viewer wonder what on earth happened and why anyone would ever need to Google that.

The character is desperately trying to destroy the evidence.

The chat is having the greatest night of its life.

And the internet has just discovered eight things it was NEVER supposed to know.
